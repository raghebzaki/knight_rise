Knight Rise Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026 · Applies to Knight Rise for Android and iOS

In short: everything stays on your device. Knight Rise has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. We never see, collect, sell or share your data.

What the app stores, and where

To remind you and show your progress, Knight Rise keeps the following only in a database on your device:

  • Your routines (hydration, stretch, eye rest), intervals and daily targets.
  • Your working hours and one-off changes such as "day off" or "working late".
  • What you completed or snoozed, and when; water amounts you log.
  • Shield (focus / meeting) sessions.
  • Daily summaries used for your streak, rank and history charts.
  • App settings (theme, haptics, streak goal, timing mode, time zone).

Detailed activity is deleted automatically after 90 days; small daily summaries are kept so your history and streaks keep working. Nothing is uploaded or backed up by Knight Rise itself. If your phone's own system backup includes app data, that backup is governed by Google's or Apple's terms, not ours.

Permissions and why

  • Notifications — to deliver your reminders. Asked only when you choose "Enable reminders".
  • Alarms & reminders (Android, optional) — only if you turn on Precision Timing, so reminders fire on the exact minute.
  • Run at startup (Android) and Background App Refresh (iOS) — to restore your reminder schedule after a restart and keep it topped up.
  • Vibration — for reminder alerts and optional haptic feedback.

Knight Rise does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, health data or internet-based services.

Children

Knight Rise is a general-audience productivity app. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
