> Stay Active. Stay Focused.
>
> Knight Rise reminds you to drink water, stand up and stretch, and rest your eyes — only during your working hours, and never in the middle of a meeting.
>
> **Reminders that respect your day**
> • Set your work hours once, with split shifts and lunch breaks. Outside them, Knight Rise stays silent.
> • Mark "Day off" or "Working late" for today with one tap.
> • Shield Mode pauses reminders for focus time or meetings, then sends one gentle summary when you're back.
>
> **Act right from the notification**
> • Tap Done or Snooze without opening the app.
> • Reminders that fall close together are merged into one, so you're never spammed.
>

> • Three rings show today's water, stretches and eye rests at a glance.

> • Rise through the ranks: Page, Squire, Knight, Paladin.
> • 7- and 30-day history charts, focus time and average water.
>
> **Private by design**
> Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
>
> **Built for everyone**
> Screen-reader labels, large-text layouts, reduced-motion support, and colours checked for colour-blind users. Dark Midnight and true-black AMOLED themes.
